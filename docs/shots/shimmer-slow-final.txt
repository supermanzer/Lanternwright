shimmer attribution (G10.0.2): a walk at 0.028 m/frame with the wind on, the game clock advancing at the walking pace, TAA on, 60 frames at 960x540.
Two metrics (0-255 luma squared; lower is steadier): SURFACE crawl is the F02 motion-compensated one (it reprojects with depth, so it skips sky, depth edges and silhouettes);
EDGE crawl is new and needs no depth: edge pixels (gradient > 24) matched against the best of 3x3 pixels of the previous frame, so it sees twigs, blade tips and crystal points against the sky.
Each layer is switched off in turn; its pixels are the ones whose luma changes by more than 12 / 255 when it is off, and its share is the part of the crawl that falls on them.
Wind moves blades and boughs for real, which counts as crawl ('clock frozen' = the same walk with the clock stopped: aliasing alone). Shares overlap (a shadow on grass counts for
both), so they can add to more than 100 %. Every walk is reproducible (exposure fixed, TAA state and jitter reset): two identical walks differ by less than 1/255 in every pixel that matters.
READ WITH CARE: ownership says where the crawl falls, not what causes it. The sky's edge share is mostly tree and glass silhouettes against the sky, counted twice (the sky pixel beside them
belongs to the sky layer, the twig to the tree). The shadows share is lighting dependence (a canopy's shade covers most of a wood), largely inherited from the trees. Glass shows 0 % surface
because the surface metric skips refractive pixels: only its edge share means anything. 'grove' is the ground-grass-5m view, which stands inside the woods (grass is judged on
the edge column and on the dock/crystal scenes, not here).
