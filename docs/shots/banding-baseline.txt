# G10.0.3 baseline. Command: Lanternwright --banding (main after G10.0.2)
banding probe (G10.0.3): the final 8-bit image of smooth sky strips, 1600x900, TAA on, 32 columns per view. levels = distinct values used by a channel in the strip; max step = the biggest change
between adjacent rows; longest run = most identical rows in a row; flat = share of adjacent rows with no change at all (a smooth ramp that uses every level has flat close to 0 where the gradient is
steep, and runs of 1 to 4 where it is shallow; bands are long runs). Dithering (G10.2.3) should raise the levels and cut the runs.
A strip is cut where adjacent rows differ by more than 3, so a max step is at most 3 by construction; 2 or 3 inside a smooth strip is itself a band edge.
