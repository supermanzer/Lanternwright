# G10.0.3 baseline. Command: Lanternwright --banding (main after G10.0.2)
banding probe (G10.0.3): the final 8-bit image of smooth sky strips, 1600x900, TAA on, up to 40 columns per view, the longest smooth sky strip in each.
HEADLINE for G10.2.3: longest run (most identical rows in a row) and flat (share of adjacent rows with no change at all). Banding = long runs and a high flat share; dither must cut both.
steps = share of adjacent rows whose largest channel step is 0 / 1 / 2 / 3+ (strips are cut at steps over 3). fill = distinct levels used / levels spanned by the strip (max - min + 1), mean over strips:
a banded ramp is already near 1 (it visits every level), so fill is context, not the target. Clouds and the sun's glow are included wherever they are smooth enough.
