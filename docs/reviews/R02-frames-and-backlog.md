## Defects seen in the frames (in priority order)

1. **Tree placement is regular and trees are identical.** In `overview` and `dock` the cypresses sit in neat contour-following rows, and every one is the same cone with the same lean and colour. Replace the placement with **Bridson Poisson-disc sampling** (blue-noise spacing, min distance varying with a density mask) driven by rules: altitude band, slope limit, distance from the path and landmarks (exclusion zones you already have), plus a low-frequency "grove vs clearing" noise so there are clusters, gaps and a few loners. Per tree: scale 0.7-1.3, small random lean (1-3 degrees), colour/value jitter, and a rare dead or fallen one. Reuse the same sampler for beach clutter and Glass Wood crystal trees. Accept: before/after `overview` shows clusters and clearings with no visible rows; deterministic under the seeded noise; exclusion zones respected (selftest).
2. **Clouds read as muddy tan smears.** In `dock`, `shore` and `summit` they are a flat ochre-brown with horizontal streaking. Real dusk clouds are lit warm (orange to pink) on the sun side, blue-grey on the far side, with brighter rims. Light them from the same atmosphere the rest of the scene uses: colour = sun colour (after transmittance) x a thin-cloud term (`exp(-density*k)` along 2-3 taps toward the sun, which also gives a silver lining) + sky ambient, never a fixed tan. Also fix the streak: the cloud plane at 2500 m stretches texture toward the horizon, so fade detail and density with distance into the horizon haze and scale the noise by `1/horizon distance` stretch. Accept: `dock` and `shore` clouds show a warm/cool split and rims, no brown cast; no visible smear at the horizon.
3. **Aliased path texture in the Glass Wood** (`gw-arrival`): the path shows hard-edged white pixel blocks, a noise threshold with no band-limiting. Use the standard fix: derive the pixel footprint with `fwidth(P)` and fade high octaves / widen the smoothstep by it, so detail resolves smoothly with distance. Check the Varrow terrain noise for the same problem. Accept: no hard pixel blocks in `gw-arrival`, `gw-court` or `dock` path at 1600x900 and at 2560x1440.
4. **Featureless near ground.** The `gw-arrival` foreground (bottom third) is one smooth purple dome, and the `summit` foreground is a dark blotchy mottle with dark outlines around the blobs (looks like contour lines from a thresholded value noise, likely the new layer blend or the damp term). Look at `summit` at the close-up camera and at `shore`; remove contour outlines (smoother blend, noise with quintic interpolation, rotate octaves) and give the Glass Wood ground the same three-scale variation Varrow got in T55.14. Also check the stepped straight-edged ledge on the `summit` skyline (top right): it looks like a pad flatten with a hard edge. Soften the pad blend or break it with the rock mask. Accept: before/after close-ups, or revert.
5. **Glass Wood grass is a uniform carpet.** Identical blade height, colour and spacing. Use the Poisson/clump noise from item 1 for clumps and bare patches, per-blade height and hue variance, and lower the density near the path and crystals. A few taller seed-head blades read as meadow rather than carpet. Accept: before/after `gw-arrival`.
6. **Beach clutter reads as stakes.** In `shore`, driftwood is thin dark sticks standing at angles and one tall dark spike stands in the grass (bottom centre). Lay driftwood flat (or nearly), partly buried, aligned roughly along the shoreline, in a few clusters on the wrack line (just above the foam edge) instead of uniformly. Make pebbles clump and vary 3x in size. Remove or shorten the stray spike. Accept: before/after `shore`.
7. **Near-water streaking** (`dock`, left): the ripples look like smeared brush strokes at grazing angles. Cross two ripple layers in different directions, domain-warp one, and fade the highest-frequency layer by the `fwidth` footprint. Fold into the water upgrade (B5) if you take it.
8. **Judgement calls for the human, not for you:** the milky horizon haze in `overview` (is the fog too thick from altitude?) and whether the dock's long beach shadow in `dock` is too hard-edged. If you think a change is warranted, put it in INSPECT.md as a question rather than changing it.
