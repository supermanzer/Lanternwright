## The world (S01 6, S02 5): a flooded ledger-observatory
- **Place**: a half-drowned observatory of terraces and galleries: shelves of ledgers rising from tidal water, a brass observatory dome, an arched sluice gate system, a prism chamber at the top. Palette: wet slate, brass, pale paper, green-grey water; warm lamplight inside. Respect the user's material rules: weathered, natural textures (no plastic rectangles), all light has a source, no shadow from light.
- **Time**: a tide cycle (about 90 s per full rise and fall, deterministic from the game clock) raises and lowers water in the galleries. The player learns that some doors and ledgers are reachable only at a given level (observable cause and effect).
- **Optical property: dispersion.** A prism chamber splits one beam into three coloured beams that map to the three tones (tide, wind, stone). The tide level and the beam's direction select which sluice each component feeds.
- **Key puzzle, two solutions (S02 3.4):** open three sluices in the right order to drain the Great Ledger gallery. Ilse's way (held tones with the tuning fork, kept from this world; tide, wind, stone struck in order of altitude and held at the right tide level); Oren's way (turn the beam through the prism with the lens so each component hits its sluice). The chosen sibling's tool makes its solution obvious; the other is discoverable.
- **New tool kept:** the tuning fork (Ilse's route) or nothing new for Oren (he has the lens): on either route, the other tool can be found in the world and teaches the other way (counts toward the optional third ending, S02 section 8 requirement 1).
- **Story beat**: what a Lanternwright is; the guild and its charter; the Roll of Keepers; **the chain shown as a map for the first time**; the lighthouse's real purpose; Ilse's sanctuary vs. the cage of rules (S02 world table row 3). Maren (the teacher) stays ambiguous (S02 decision 3).
- **Pages**: 6 new pages (IDs 10-15) in the chosen route's voice, 3 of them also exist in the other sibling's voice as found-object interpretations; all overlookable (F03 #43); the gate for leaving is **the solved puzzle only**, never a count of pages. Page text drafts go to `docs/plans/S04-page-drafts.md` for the reviewer to read; apply after reading (the user has twice reacted to wording).
- **Shadow-play tableau (optional)**: one lantern lit at the right place casts the remembered moment on a wall.
- **Contradictions**: one new evidence slot from the Roll of Keepers (optional; never says wrong; never gates).
