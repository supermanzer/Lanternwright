# S03 player path: what a player must discover, in order, and what tells them

Written 2026-10-29 by builder B for F06 (B2), **from the code**, not from the plans. It answers user note #67 ("unclear what the mechanics are the player must undertake"). The reviewer uses it to judge legibility. Every "what tells them" cell names the thing in the game that does the telling (a line of text, a sound, a thing that moves or lights), and the file it comes from. "None" means nothing in the game says it.

The whole path is played, from a fresh game, by `SelfTestBots` (`bot:` checks: dock, stones, causeway, Glass Wood, return, note, plates, door, stair, lens room, both exits, saves). A player who has not read any plan is assumed. There is no timer and no way to lose (`Controls.reassurance`).

**Verdict key:** **Clear** = the game says or shows it. **Hinted** = a player who looks and reads will get there; one who does not may stall. **Unclear** = nothing says it; the player must guess or experiment.

## E. Things the player may use but never has to

The Notebook's rubbings and "tap to hear" (replay a struck stone), the door-note line, the optional contradiction card (pages 4, 5, 8, 9 against "Hers: He left me." / "His: She would not follow."), the journal's tabs by author.

## Gaps for the reviewer to judge (in the order they would stall a player)

Closed in F06 B2b (Q22, Q23): the plates are no longer unintroduced (the door's second line, the glint); the ring and the lens speak differently and the ring turns either way; the developer suffix and the cut-off account are gone. What remains:

1. **Closed in F06 B3 (Q24):** the lens and the receiver each take a light (warm beads, a cool rim) while the gate is ready, so the two exits read as two things to use. Not coupled to the answer phrase's sound (that plays outside, near the door, not in the lens room).
2. **A player who has not found the ring may still not connect it to the receiver's rim;** the beam preview from the ring to the receiver does that once the ring is touched.
3. **The chime pool relies on memory (step 10).** The Notebook can replay the tones but nothing in the Glass Wood points back to it.
4. **Page 5's spot (step 8).** Kept; the squares say a page exists in the Glass Wood.
5. **The lamp's line (step 13)** is never said if the player leaves the Glass Wood within 18 s of the altar (harmless: the ring is lit and the way back is the only exit).

## What the bot proves, and what it does not

The bot proves that every step above is reachable with the real controller and real clicks, in order, from a fresh game: the dock, pages 9 and 1 to 4, the stones, the causeway, the lens, the Glass Wood's pages, mirrors, chimes, the altar, the return, the note card, the plates (standing in front of each: from the side along the row, the near plate hides the far one, so a player must face them), the open door, the stair, the lens room, Ilse's exit, and (from a save made in the lens room) Oren's exit by three ring touches and the doorway; and that after a save and a load each route is kept and the other exit is still sealed. It does NOT prove a player would do any of it unprompted: that is the "Unclear" and "Hinted" cells above.
