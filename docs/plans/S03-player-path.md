# S03 player path: what a player must discover, in order, and what tells them

Written 2026-10-29 by builder B for F06 (B2), **from the code**, not from the plans. It answers user note #67 ("unclear what the mechanics are the player must undertake"). The reviewer uses it to judge legibility. Every "what tells them" cell names the thing in the game that does the telling (a line of text, a sound, a thing that moves or lights), and the file it comes from. "None" means nothing in the game says it.

The whole path is played, from a fresh game, by `SelfTestBots` (`bot:` checks: dock, stones, causeway, Glass Wood, return, note, plates, door, stair, lens room, both exits, saves). A player who has not read any plan is assumed. There is no timer and no way to lose (`Controls.reassurance`).

**Verdict key:** **Clear** = the game says or shows it. **Hinted** = a player who looks and reads will get there; one who does not may stall. **Unclear** = nothing says it; the player must guess or experiment.

**Gaps for the reviewer in Part 2 (in the order they would stall a player):**
1. **The visible water is everything (steps 35 and 36).** The code makes the tide, the windows and the failures exact; whether a player can SEE which gate the water is "at" is builder A's S04.2 and S04.3 (marks on the gates, the water line reaching them).
