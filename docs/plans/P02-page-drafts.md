## Page 6 (Oren, the Glass Wood receiver): the mirror court no longer works as the page says
*Live:* "Light is not carried, Ilse. It is turned. Three mirrors, three panes of glass: I set them by the order on the wall, the way the keeper climbs. Water, wind, stone. When the beam arrives in that order, the wood answers."
*Why:* since P02.1 the court is solved when all three crystals are lit at once, in no order; the page tells the player a rule that is not the court's. It is also one of the G10 pins (page 6 states the order for the plates).
*Draft A (court only, drops the order):* "Light is not carried, Ilse. It is turned. Three mirrors, three panes of glass, and three crystals that will not wake for the wrong colour. I turned and changed them until all three were awake at once, and the wood answered."
*Draft B (keeps a nod to the stones, no order):* "Light is not carried, Ilse. It is turned. Each crystal here keeps one of the three colours we learned on the island, and wants only that one. The wood answers when none of them is left asleep."
Note: if page 6 stops stating the order, the plates lose a clue source (G8 clue budget) until P02.3 gives them the beam's rhythm.

## Page 13 (the Archive, Ilse's voice): states the gates' order
*Live:* "The sluices answer to a tone and to the water together. Tide first, because everything begins at the water. Then wind. Then stone. ..."
*Why:* G10 (and, since P02.5, G8 for `archive-gates-tone`: with the three-heights key this is a third clue source). The plan's row says it should stop stating the answer outright and let the gate marks carry a level line (builder A's marks, Q76).
