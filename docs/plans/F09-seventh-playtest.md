### F09.4 Sound (#90, #91, #92, #108; owner: A, `Audio/**`)
User principle (#91): "Ground both these sounds in the properties of the physical things they represent." Same method as F07 door sound: model the physics, then judge bands in a selftest and listen to the numbers (nobody can hear it but the user, so say plainly it is untested by ear).
1. **Shingle footstep (#90)** "like muddy boots": shingle is many small hard stones: a short burst of 15-40 grain clicks (each a 1-3 ms decaying resonance 2-6 kHz, random per grain), a low crunch body of band-passed noise, NO low-frequency thump or wet sound; heel-toe two-part for a walk. The current stock is probably low-passed noise with a thud. Keep the -4 dB level from F07.
2. **Steep-grade sound (#91a)** "loud static": this is the scree/rock-slide or slip-scrape layer. On steep rock/earth it should be sparse loose-stone clacks and soil scuffs triggered by foot slip speed, with a level that follows slip speed, not a continuous noise bed. Cap loudness well below footsteps.
3. **Wind (#91b)** "a strange sine wave of distortion". Wind is turbulence: broadband noise shaped by 1/f (pink-ish) with slow gust envelopes (several seconds, gamma-distributed, correlated with the visible wind), and resonance only where an object makes one (a gap, wire, tree crowns). The filter centre must move with gust strength and must NOT be a high-Q sweep (that is the sine). Make hills shelter (lower and darker when the player is in a lee), and leaves/grass hiss louder where there are trees/grass underfoot. Selftest: a spectral flatness / peak-to-median check so no narrow peak above 10 dB over the one-third-octave neighbours (as the door test does).
4. **Tones go out of tune on approach to the lighthouse (#92)** "The tones go out of tune after the initial ring as we approach the lighthouse." Almost certainly a Doppler/pitch effect on the distance model, a resampling rate tied to the moving listener, or the beat between the stone tones and the lighthouse drone. Tones that are puzzle answers (the stone tones are the Glass Wood chime solution) must be pitch-stable: remove any listener-velocity pitch shift from them, and check the drone does not detune them. Selftest: sample the tone's fundamental at 5 distances while walking at pace: within 3 cents.
5. **Water on stone (#108)**: "standing at least partway in the water my footsteps sound like dry sand; should splash." Position (52.3, 11.0, -27.0) is the Archive's stone gallery floor under tide water. Footstep surface must be decided by water depth at the foot first, then by material (ankle = splash on stone, shin = wade). The Archive's tide level is B's `TideArchivePuzzle`; read the depth from the same function the water render uses. Selftest at the three tide levels.
