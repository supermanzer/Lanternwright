## What the user's playtest added (2026-10-28, notes #58, #59)

- #58: "One thing is not clear to the player after reviewing both siblings' stories: where should the player head next? If we are meant to return to the island, we should make it more clear."
- #59: "Upon returning to the island, the lighthouse is the clear focal point. This should be where the player can determine which path to follow and begin exploring other worlds through one sibling's perspective."
- Standing: #43/F03 P1 said pages must be overlookable ("pay attention to details"), and #56 asked to restore earlier wording of a page. These **override S02's hard requirement that every critical-path page is impossible to miss**. The gate therefore must not depend on finding every page (it needs 3 of each author, S02 section 2.1), and the game must tell the player *what is missing*, quietly, by author (S02 2.1: "You have heard only one of them").

## Design consequences (reviewer's proposals; the user may correct with F)

1. **The return is announced and led.** When the Glass Wood altar is solved and the player returns to Varrow (the lens ring), the arrival is at the lens islet as now; then **the lighthouse becomes the focal point**: its beam changes (a slow double sweep, warmer lamp, one steady beam toward the player's position every cycle), the lighthouse path's lanterns come on one after another along the nav trail (the corridors from M10 already lead there), a low chime phrase (the answer motif from `ToneTable`) plays once on arrival and again whenever the player is within 40 m of the door; the corridor margin never blocks it. No marker, no text prompt beyond one journal line ("The lamp is waiting."), per the design pillars.
2. **The lighthouse is where the choice is made.** The choice is one place, a **threshold in the lens room**, not two scattered puzzles:
   - **Ilse's way (tone plates, S02 2.2):** the three plates beside the door; when answered, the door opens, the interior is entered, and **at the lens ring on top the player may begin Ilse's route**.
   - **Oren's way (turned beam, S02 2.3):** the **lens ring itself can be turned** (it is Oren's "turning lens"): from the lens room the player rotates it until the sweep beam falls on the dock arch (visible through the lens windows) and the arch opens; the shore mirror stands become an **optional** simplification (they remain in the plan for the dock-side approach, and the receiver on the arch is the same). *This moves the decisive act for Oren's exit from the shore into the lens room, so both exits are decided in the lighthouse; the portal itself stays at the dock (S02 2.3). If the user prefers the shore version, only the control moves.*
   - Whichever is completed first is **sealed for good** (S02 section 1), the other exit is visibly closed (the plates go dark / the arch stays dim), as decided.
3. **The gate is always legible** (S02 2.1, with the user's #58 in mind): at the lens room, the ring shows the gate's state by three small lamps (Glass Wood solved; Ilse's pages; Oren's pages) and one notebook line says what is missing in the sibling's own idiom, never a quest marker. Nothing blocks a player who has what the gate asks for.
