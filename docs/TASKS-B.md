New: `SelfTests/SelfTestGrassCull.swift` (+ one registration in SelfTest.swift), `scripts/grass-frames.sh`, `scripts/grass-bench.sh`.
  **How it works.** One threadgroup per row of the 271 x 271 grid. Each cell is tested cheapest first, only ever REJECTING and only with a margin: distance and the hash against the distance fade, then the view frustum (a margin of 10 m covers a blade's height and lean), then the terrain's no-grass zones, then the density with its noise. Survivors are compacted in the OLD instance order (a count per row, each row's offset, a copy: no atomics), so equal-depth ties resolve as before. The vertex shader still runs its own tests on every cell it is given and is the final arbiter. 6 to 10 % of the cells survive (4,400 to 7,700 of 73,441); the culling kernel's density terms are a COPY of the vertex shader's (moving that code into a shared function changed the Glass Wood frame `gw-court` by a few bits under fast-math, so it was reverted).

The selftest renders 16 cameras (all the way round, straight down, up, eye at the ground, both worlds) with and without the culling and requires byte-identical frames. **Mutation check:** with the frustum margin at 0 the test fails (1 of 16 identical); at 3 and 6 m it still passes (10 m has headroom).
